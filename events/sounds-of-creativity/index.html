Sounds of Creativity

What is Sounds of Creativity?

Sounds of Creativity is a Design-a-thon event where participants are challenged to design a character based on a song prompt.

Each participant is assigned an individual song design prompt. Participants have one week to complete and submit their event design entry. Additional shared daily song design prompts will be posted availble to all participants on the Sounds of Creativity event page. Designs MUST be inspired by the design prompt. This can be through accessories, color palette, patterns, etc. Participants may opt to have their design submissions displayed on the Sounds of Creativity event page.

Participants earn 15 tickets for completing and submitting an event prompt design and 5 tickets for completing and submitting a daily prompt design. Tickets may be used to purchase art and design commissions in other participant's shops.
