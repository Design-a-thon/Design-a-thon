What is Design-a-thon?

Design-a-thon (DAT) is a biannual online art event currently hosted by @Lemon24K, focused on the creation and trading of original character designs. DAT aims to foster a thriving artist community that uplifts artists and helps them discover the love for creating original characters.

How does Design-a-thon work?

Design-a-thon consists of three uniquely themed main events which are described in more detail on the Events page. During each week-long event, participants receive an individual prompt that they use to design an original character. In addition, shared daily prompts are available for all participants throughout each event. Completed submissions are then showcased on the event homepage.

Submission of event entries grants Design-a-thon participants tickets. Tickets are the virtual Design-a-thon currency. Design-a-thon participants are encouraged to host shops to sell art and design commissions to other Design-a-thon participants. Tickets can be used to purchase items from Design-a-thon participant shops.

How can I join?

You may sign-up for Design-a-thon during the designated registration period which opens shortly before the start of the overall Design-a-thon event. Registration is free and open to all artists. Generally, registration opens in December for the Winter Design-a-thon and in July for the Summer Design-a-thon.

Sign-ups for the current Summer26 Design-a-thon are open! You may sign-up on the Scratch sign-up project.

Design-a-thon History

The concept of Design-a-thon was created by @infernal-qhost . The first Design-a-thon event was hosted by @infernal-qhost and @Lemon24K in Winter 2021 on Scratch. Since then, Design-a-thon has been held biannually with a winter event and a spring event each year. Events since Summer 2023 were hosted by @Lemon24K .
