Sweet Shop

What is Sweet Shop?

Sweet Shop is a Design-a-thon event where participants are challenged to design a character based on a sweet treat. Depending on whether it is the summer or winter Design-a-thon event, the sweet treat prompts will be summer or winter themed as well.

Each participant is assigned an individual sweet treat design prompt. Participants have one week to complete and submit their event design entry. Additional shared daily sweet treat design prompts will be posted availble to all participants on the Sweet Shop event page. Designs MUST be inspired by the design prompt. This can be through accessories, color palette, patterns, etc. Participants may opt to have their design submissions displayed on the Sweet Shop event page.

Participants earn 15 tickets for completing and submitting an event prompt design and 5 tickets for completing and submitting a daily prompt design. Tickets may be used to purchase art and design commissions in other participant's shops.
