What are Design-a-thon Events?

Design-a-thon events are week-long challenges where participants receive an individual prompt that they use to create an original design. Prompts are generated based on the theme of the event and can include color palette prompts, item prompts, aesthetic prompts, and more. In addition, shared daily prompts are available for all participants throughout each event. Completed submissions are then showcased on the event homepage.

Some of the past Design-a-thon events include:

  • Sweet Shop
  • Sounds of Creativity
  • Combination Domination
  • Beasts of Fantasy
  • Color Splash
  • Aesthetic Harmony
  • Design-a-palooza
