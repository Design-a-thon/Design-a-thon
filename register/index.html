Design-a-thon History

The concept of Design-a-thon was created by @infernal-qhost . The first Design-a-thon event was hosted by @infernal-qhost and @Lemon24K in Winter 2021 on Scratch. Since then, Design-a-thon has been held biannually with a winter event and a spring event each year. Events since Summer 2023 were hosted by @Lemon24K .
