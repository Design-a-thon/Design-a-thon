What are Design-a-thon Shops?

Design-a-thon shops are virtual marketplaces where Design-a-thon participants can buy and sell their art commissions with tickets. These shops are hosted by participants during Design-a-thon serve as a platform for creators to showcase their art and designs and connect with potential buyers.
